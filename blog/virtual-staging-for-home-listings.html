How Virtual Staging Helps Buyers Visualize Your Home

Furniture can show buyers how a room is laid out and what might fit inside it. Virtual staging adds that context to photos of empty or outdated rooms.

What Is Virtual Staging?

Virtual staging uses 3D rendering software to digitally place photorealistic furniture, rugs, art, and accessories into photos of empty or outdated rooms. We deliver MLS-ready images within 48 hours.

Show the Scale and Use of a Room

A sofa, rug or dining table gives buyers a sense of scale. A staged photo can also show how a room might function, such as where seating or a work area could fit.

When Virtual Staging Makes Sense

  • The property is vacant and physical staging isn't in the budget
  • You need MLS-ready photos within 48 hours
  • You want to present multiple style options to appeal to different buyer demographics
  • The home is already furnished but outdated, and you need a "refreshed" look for photography
