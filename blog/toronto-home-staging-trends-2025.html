5 Home Staging Trends Seen in Toronto in 2025

Here are five home staging trends our team observed across the GTA in 2025, with ways to use them in listing photos and showings.

1. Warm Earthy Palettes

Cool greys and stark whites have given way to terracotta, warm taupe, and soft clay tones. These colours make spaces feel lived-in and inviting — exactly the emotion that converts browsers into buyers. We pair them with natural linen throws and rattan accents for instant warmth.

2. Scandi-Minimalism with Soul

Clean lines remain popular, but buyers want personality. The trick is layering — a minimal sofa with a chunky knit cushion, a sleek shelf with one statement sculpture. Less clutter, more intention.

3. Biophilic Design

Statement plants such as fiddle-leaf figs, monstera or olive trees can add shape and colour to a room. They add life to listing photos and signal that the space is well cared for. We typically introduce 3–5 plants per home depending on size.

4. Statement Lighting

Builder-grade ceiling fixtures are swapped for sculptural pendants and arc floor lamps. Good lighting photographs beautifully and elevates even modest rooms from "fine" to "feature."

5. Curved Furniture

Straight edges are out; organic silhouettes are in. Curved sofas and rounded side tables soften living areas and photograph with an editorial quality that flat-edged pieces simply can't match.
