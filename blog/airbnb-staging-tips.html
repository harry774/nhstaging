Staging Your Airbnb for Listing Photos and Guest Comfort

Your Airbnb photos can show guests how the property is laid out and what they can expect during a stay. Here is what our team focuses on when staging short-term rentals.

First Impressions Start Online

Your listing thumbnail is competing against hundreds of others. A hero shot of a beautifully staged living room — with layered textiles, ambient lighting, and thoughtful accessories — stops the scroll. We design AirBnB spaces with photography in mind from the start.

Comfort Sells

Guests want to imagine themselves relaxing, not just sleeping. Plush throw pillows, quality bedding, a well-arranged reading nook, or a styled kitchen counter communicates care and quality. These details make it easier for guests to picture using the space.

Key Areas We Focus On

Bedroom: Hotel-quality bedding, blackout curtains, and layered pillows. A bedside lamp on each side. One piece of art that anchors the room.

Living Area: A focal point (fireplace, TV unit, or feature wall), comfortable seating, a statement rug, and enough plants to add life without clutter.

Kitchen & Dining: A styled dining table (even a simple centrepiece), clear countertops with one or two attractive appliances, and coordinated dishware visible through cabinet glass.

Show What Guests Will Find

Photograph the completed rooms from angles that show the layout and amenities. Update your listing photos when you change furnishings or the facilities guests can use.
