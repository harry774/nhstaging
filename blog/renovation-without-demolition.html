How to Refresh a Room Without Demolition

If you want to refresh a room without changing the layout, start with the furnishings, finishes and lighting you see and use every day.

Start With the Existing Room

If the layout works for you, changing furniture placement, colour, texture and lighting can change how the room feels.

What We Change Instead

Accent Furniture: A new sofa, side table, or statement chair can redefine an entire room's mood. We shop for pieces that balance proportion, colour, and style to create a cohesive look.

Wallpaper & Paint: One feature wall with a bold wallpaper or a fresh coat of paint in a carefully chosen tone transforms a room instantly.

Wall Art & Mirrors: Art anchors a room and gives it personality. Mirrors add light and the illusion of space. We curate pieces that complement the home's existing architecture.

Curtains & Textiles: Floor-to-ceiling curtains make ceilings look taller. The right rug defines zones in open-plan spaces. Cushions and throws add warmth in listing photos and open houses.

What a Kitchen Refresh Can Include

Consider new cabinet hardware, a peel-and-stick backsplash, fresh paint or updated lighting. The scope depends on the condition of your finishes and which features you want to keep.
