How Toronto Realtors Can Work with a Home Staging Team

A staging team can help Toronto realtors prepare properties for listing photos and showings. Here is how we work with agents and sellers.

Prepare the Home for Listing Photos

Arrange furniture to show how buyers could use each room, choose a focal point for photos, and leave clear routes through the home for showings.

What a Staging Partnership Looks Like

We work directly with your sellers — you make the introduction and we handle the rest. From the initial walk-through and mood board consultation to furniture placement and final styling on photo day, our team manages the entire process. You show up to photography with a property that's ready to impress.

Virtual Staging for Every Listing Budget

Not every seller has the budget for full physical staging. Our virtual staging service — starting at $299 — gives every listing professional-quality MLS photos, regardless of whether the property is furnished or vacant. This levels the playing field and makes every listing look premium.
